# Regional cuisine map and seasonal summary (EatOS)

Assumptions: the 7 metros are Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune (swap if EatOS differs). Dishes are home food. Not web-verifiable line by line; drawn from standard regional references. Seasonal detail is in seasonal.json; festival detail in calendar.json.
Format per entry: dishes (12) | grain | fat | souring | meal structure | dietary rules.

## Metros
### Delhi-NCR (Punjabi-UP blend)
- Dishes: dal tadka, rajma, chole, aloo gobi, baingan bharta, kadhi, aloo paratha, stuffed paratha, mixed veg, palak paneer, lauki sabzi, jeera rice
- Grain: wheat roti, rice | Fat: ghee, mustard oil, refined oil | Sour: amchur, tomato, curd, lemon
- Structure: breakfast paratha/poha; lunch roti-dal-sabzi-salad-curd; evening chai + snack; dinner lighter roti-sabzi
- Rules: Hindu vegetarian majority; Tue/Thu/Sat veg-only for many; Navratri and Ekadashi fasts; Jain (Agarwal/Jain) no onion-garlic; halal for Muslims

### Mumbai (Maharashtrian-Gujarati-Goan-Parsi mix)
- Dishes: poha, upma, misal pav, pav bhaji, vada pav, varan-bhaat, bharli vangi, thalipeeth, dal-khichdi, usal, sabudana khichdi, bombil/pomfret fry
- Grain: rice, wheat chapati, bhakri, pav | Fat: groundnut oil, ghee, coconut oil | Sour: kokum, tamarind, tomato, lemon
- Structure: poha/sheera breakfast; dabba lunch (roti, sabzi, dal-rice); evening snack; dinner rice-based
- Rules: heavy Jain/Gujarati vegetarian (Jain no root vegetables, no sunset eating in Chaturmas/Paryushan); coastal non-veg; Shravan veg; Parsi eggs/meat; no beef/pork in many communities

### Pune
- Dishes: poha, sabudana khichdi, misal, thalipeeth, pithla-bhakri, usal-pav, amti-bhat, batata bhaji-puri, aamras-puri (summer), shrikhand, zunka, kothimbir vadi
- Grain: jowar, bajra, wheat, rice | Fat: groundnut oil, ghee | Sour: kokum, tamarind, curd, lemon
- Structure: breakfast poha/upma; lunch bhakri/poli, bhaji, varan-bhaat; evening chivda; early dinner
- Rules: Brahmin/Marathi veg homes avoid onion-garlic on festival days; Shravan Mondays and Ashadhi Ekadashi big; non-veg common in CKP and Konkani communities

### Bengaluru (Kannadiga plus Tamil, Telugu, Malayali migrants)
- Dishes: idli-sambar, dosa, ragi mudde-saaru, bisi bele bath, akki roti, puliyogare, vangi bath, rasam-rice, curd rice, upma, chitranna, tomato bath
- Grain: rice, ragi, jowar | Fat: coconut/sunflower oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind, tomato, kokum, lemon
- Structure: big breakfast (idli/dosa/pongal); rice-sambar-rasam-curd lunch; filter coffee; light dinner (chapati, dosa, ragi)
- Rules: Brahmin/Lingayat vegetarian; non-veg in coastal Karnataka (Mangalore) and Kodava (pork); Ekadashi, Ugadi, Ganesh fasts

### Hyderabad
- Dishes: dal-rice, pappu-annam, gongura pachadi, tomato-pappu, bagara baingan, mirchi ka salan, khichdi-kheema, biryani (weekends), haleem (Ramzan), sambar-rice, sheer khurma, double ka meetha
- Grain: rice, jowar roti, wheat | Fat: groundnut/sesame oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind, gongura, raw mango, tomato, lemon
- Structure: breakfast idli/dosa/upma/poori; lunch rice-based; dinner roti-curry or biryani
- Rules: Muslim halal (dominant Ramzan food culture), Telugu Hindu vegetarian days (Saturday), no beef for Hindus

### Chennai
- Dishes: idli, dosa, pongal, sambar, rasam, kootu, poriyal, curd rice, lemon rice, puliyodarai, vada, filter coffee
- Grain: rice, ragi | Fat: gingelly oil, coconut oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind, tomato, lemon, raw mango, kokum
- Structure: iddli/dosa breakfast; full rice meal with sambar-rasam-curd; tiffin at 4pm; light dinner of dosa/idli/chapathi
- Rules: Iyer/Iyengar vegetarian no onion-garlic on shraadham/festivals; Saturday/Tuesday non-veg avoided in many homes; Karthigai, Navaratri golu, Pongal

### Kolkata
- Dishes: macher jhol, shorshe ilish, aloo posto, begun bhaja, luchi, khichuri, dal-bhaat, cholar dal, shukto, mishti doi, posto bora, alu dum
- Grain: rice | Fat: mustard oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind (tetul), tomato, lemon, kul (jujube), amra
- Structure: luchi-alu or ruti-torkari breakfast; bhaat-dal-bhaja-torkari-maachh lunch; dinner roti and curry; fish daily
- Rules: fish-eating Brahmins; Vaishnava and widow-tradition niramish (vegetarian) cooking; no onion-garlic for Vaishnavas; Durga Puja bhog without onion-garlic

## Home regions
### Gujarat
- Dishes: dal-bhat-rotli-shaak, khichdi-kadhi, thepla, dhokla, khandvi, handvo, undhiyu, sev tameta, bhakri-ringan, muthia, khichu, fafda-jalebi
- Grain: wheat, bajra, rice | Fat: groundnut oil, ghee | Sour: kokum, tomato, curd, lemon, amchur
- Structure: farsan breakfast; thali lunch (dal, shaak, kadhi, rice, rotli); light dinner (khichdi-kadhi); chaas
- Rules: Jain (no root veg, no night food); Swaminarayan (no onion-garlic); largely vegetarian; sweet in dal

### Rajasthan
- Dishes: dal-baati-churma, gatte ki sabzi, kair-sangri, bajra roti-lasun chutney, laal maas, pyaaz kachori, ker-sangri, mangodi, kadhi-pakora, ghevar (festive), moong dal halwa, mirchi vada
- Grain: bajra, wheat, jowar | Fat: ghee, mustard oil | Sour: amchur, curd, tamarind, lemon
- Structure: kachori breakfast; thali lunch with bajra; dinner dal-roti; ghee-rich
- Rules: Marwari/Jain vegetarian no onion-garlic; Rajput non-veg (laal maas); Sundays meat

### Punjab
- Dishes: sarson saag-makki, rajma-chawal, chole kulche, dal makhani, kadhi chawal, paratha-dahi-lassi, paneer bhurji, butter chicken, aloo gobi, kadhi pakora, kheer, lassi
- Grain: wheat, rice | Fat: ghee, butter, mustard oil | Sour: amchur, tomato, curd, anardana
- Structure: big paratha-lassi breakfast; roti-dal-sabzi lunch; evening chai-pakora; heavy dinner
- Rules: Sikh langar vegetarian, no halal/ritual meat (kutha); Hindu Punjabi veg on Tues/Thu; alcohol culture

### UP / Bihar
- Dishes: litti-chokha, dal-bhat-sabzi, kadhi-bari, aloo-puri, sattu paratha, chana ghugni, thekua, tehri, matar kachori, bedmi-puri, khichdi (Sankranti), dal pitha
- Grain: wheat, rice | Fat: mustard oil, ghee | Sour: amchur, tomato, tamarind, lemon, kachri
- Structure: kachori-jalebi/puri-sabzi breakfast; dal-bhat-sabzi-chokha lunch; roti-dal dinner; sattu in summer
- Rules: Brahmin vegetarianism; Chhath satvik without onion-garlic; Tuesday/Navratri; Muslim Awadhi non-veg (kebab, korma)

### Bengal (home region)
- Dishes: bhaat-dal-maachher jhol, shukto, posto, kosha mangsho-luchi, begun bhaja, dhokar dalna, chingri malai curry, pantha bhat-ilish, khichuri, payesh, mishti doi, rosogolla
- Grain: rice | Fat: mustard oil, ghee | Sour: tetul, lemon, tomato, amshotto | Rules: see Kolkata; Poila Boishakh, Durga Puja feast

### Odisha
- Dishes: dalma, pakhala bhata, santula, saga bhaja, bara-ghuguni, chhena poda, chhena jhili, dahi-baigana, machha besara, khichdi (Puri mahaprasad), rasabali, pitha
- Grain: rice | Fat: mustard oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind, tomato, amba (mango), tentuli
- Structure: pakhala summer lunch; rice-dalma-santula-saga; dinner light
- Rules: Jagannath Mahaprasad strictly onion-garlic-free and potato/tomato-free; fish common; Ekadashi fasting

### Assam and Northeast
- Dishes: masor tenga, khar, aloo pitika, duck curry, bamboo shoot pork, jadoh, dohneiiong, thukpa, momo, eromba, axone pork, pitha
- Grain: rice (sticky), millets | Fat: mustard oil | Sour: elephant apple, lemon, tomato, bamboo shoot, outenga
- Structure: rice-based thrice daily; fermented/smoked foods; tea
- Rules: largely non-vegetarian; Vaishnava (satra) vegetarian pockets; beef/pork present; fermented foods strong smell

### Maharashtra
- Dishes: poha, varan-bhaat, aamti, usal, bharli vangi, bhakri-pithla, thalipeeth, zunka, puran poli, shrikhand, kolhapuri chicken, fish thali
- Grain: jowar, bajra, rice, wheat | Fat: groundnut oil, ghee | Sour: kokum, tamarind, tomato, lemon
- Structure: poha/upma breakfast; lunch varan-bhaat-bhaji; evening snack; dinner chapati
- Rules: Brahmin/Jain vegetarian, Shravan and Ekadashi observed; Konkan seafood; Kolhapuri spicy

### Goa
- Dishes: xacuti, sorpotel, vindaloo, fish curry-rice, bebinca, sannas, rawa-fried fish, caldeen, ambot tik, prawn balchão, dodol, poi
- Grain: rice, poi bread | Fat: coconut oil, coconut | Sour: kokum, tamarind, vinegar (toddy), raw mango
- Structure: rice and fish curry daily; poi breakfast
- Rules: Catholic Lent and Fridays; Hindu Konkani vegetarian days; beef/pork usage varies by community; Christmas feasting

### Karnataka (home region)
- Dishes: bisi bele bath, ragi mudde, jowar roti-ennegayi, akki roti, kosambari, majjige huli, vangi bath, neer dosa, kori rotti, chitranna, holige, mysore pak
- Grain: ragi, jowar, rice | Fat: groundnut/coconut oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind, kokum, raw mango, tomato
- Structure: tiffin breakfast; ragi mudde lunch; light dinner
- Rules: Lingayat vegetarian; coastal non-veg; Brahmin onion-garlic-free on festival days

### Kerala
- Dishes: puttu-kadala, appam-stew, avial, thoran, sambar, meen curry, olan, kootu curry, parotta-beef/egg, kappa-meen curry, payasam, sadya
- Grain: rice (matta), tapioca | Fat: coconut oil | Sour: kudampuli, tamarind, raw mango, tomato
- Structure: puttu/appam breakfast; rice-sadya lunch; kanji-payar dinner common; tea with snacks
- Rules: Hindu Namboodiri vegetarian; Christian, Muslim non-veg (beef widely); Onam sadya veg; Lent in Syrian Christians

### Tamil Nadu (home region)
- Dishes: idli, dosa, sambar, rasam, keerai kootu, curd rice, pongal, kuzhambu, vazhaipoo poriyal, murukku, payasam, filter coffee
- Grain: rice, millets | Fat: gingelly oil, coconut oil | Sour: tamarind, tomato, kokum, lemon
- Structure: see Chennai
- Rules: Iyer/Iyengar vegetarian; non-veg (Chettinad) common; Tuesday/Friday meat avoided

### Andhra and Telangana
- Dishes: pappu-annam, gongura mutton, pesarattu, avakaya pachadi, gutti vankaya, pulihora, tomato pappu, kodi vepudu, ragi sangati, sarva pindi, bobbatlu, jonna roti
- Grain: rice, jowar, ragi | Fat: groundnut oil, sesame oil, ghee | Sour: tamarind, gongura, raw mango, tomato
- Structure: breakfast tiffin; rice lunch with pickle (ghee); dinner rice or roti
- Rules: spice level highest; Saturday veg; Muslim Hyderabadi halal; Brahmin satvik on festivals

### Kashmir
- Dishes: rogan josh, haak-rice, dum aloo, yakhni, gushtaba, rista, nadru (lotus stem) yakhni, modur pulao, kahwa, sheer chai, harissa, monji haakh
- Grain: rice, wheat (bread: girda) | Fat: mustard oil, ghee | Sour: yoghurt, dried fruit, anardana
- Structure: noon chai with bread breakfast; rice-meat lunch; wazwan feast
- Rules: Muslim halal; Pandit Kashmiri Hindus eat meat, no onion-garlic in cooking (use hing, fennel, dry ginger); Navreh

## Seasonal produce quick guide (full month-by-month in seasonal.json)
- Dec-Feb winter: peas, cauliflower, carrot, radish, sarson/methi/palak/bathua, oranges, kinnow, guava, amla; tomatoes cheapest.
- Mar-Jun summer: lauki, tinda, tori, karela, cucumber, raw mango, watermelon, muskmelon, mangoes peak in May; Alphonso Apr-May (west), Dasheri/Langra Jun (north).
- Jul-Sep monsoon: bhindi, corn, arbi, pumpkin, brinjal; leafy greens costly and spoilage-prone; fish bans in west coast; pear, plum, apple, pomegranate start.
- Oct-Nov post-monsoon: cauliflower returns, singhara, sweet potato, guava, custard apple; tomato rising; festival sweets season.
- Coastal: hilsa Aug-Oct (Bengal); sardine/mackerel Sep-Mar; drumstick Feb-Apr (south); nolen gur Dec-Feb (Bengal).

- Calendar dates: Hindu festival dates verified by web search for: Sharad Navratri Oct 11, Dussehra Oct 20, Karva Chauth Oct 29, Diwali Nov 8 (2026), Shivratri Mar 6, Holi Mar 22, Gudi Padwa Apr 7, Ram Navami Apr 15, Janmashtami Aug 25, Ganesh Chaturthi Sep 4, Onam Sep 12 (2027), Pongal Jan 15, Paryushan Aug 27-Sep 3 (2027).
- Ekadashi dates and 2027 Sharad Navratri/Diwali/Karva Chauth were computed from astronomical tithi at Delhi sunrise and not cross-checked with a printed panchang; treat +/- 1 day.
- Islamic 2027 dates are projections (Ramzan Feb 8/9, Eid al-Fitr Mar 9/10, Eid al-Adha May 16/17); India follows local moon sighting, often one day later than UAE.
- Regional dish lists, produce calendar, nutritional numbers are expert knowledge, unverified against live sources.
