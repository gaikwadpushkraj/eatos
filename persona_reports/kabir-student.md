# Kabir (22, Pune hostel, no kitchen) - persona test of EatOS
Setup: vegetarian, spice 2, kitchen 'none', Playwright at 390px, clock pinned to 2026-10-14. Source read: packages/core/src/{india,recommend,aspire}.ts, drivers/receipt.ts.

## Verdict
1. The no-kitchen filter is honoured on Today/Ask/Plan, but the pool is only 12 no-cook dishes (9 reachable in practice), so he sees the same 4 dishes every week and dinner is Curd rice every night.
2. Everything built on top (wishes, taste cards, grocery list, receipts, menus) ignores the kitchen setting or Indian reality: alternatives need a stove, grocery asks for mustard seeds and curry leaves, prices show as pound signs.
3. Tone is mostly kind and un-naggy, but Maggi, whey, energy drinks, chips and cafe food are not in his world at all, so by day 3 he would stop opening it.

## What delighted him
- Tone: zero guilt on a skipped breakfast, no streaks, no shaming; fasting buttons are neutral.
- "I already ate this" and "Done" are one tap; Today is simple; works offline with 0 console errors.
- Quick Add understands "eggs 6, maggi 4" in one box and shows a preview he can fix.
- Receipt import shrugged off "Handling fee", "Order #" and rupee signs; re-import is safe (idempotent).
- 'cheap protein' correctly surfaces sprouts, eggs, roasted chana; dishes like masala chaas, murmura bhel, chikki-adjacent items feel desi.

## Missing for him
- Maggi (and any Maggi-hack), instant noodles with egg via kettle, bread omelette, Maggi-with-veggies.
- Canteen/mess awareness: "what to pick from the mess thali", "mess is paneer again, skip".
- Quick-commerce suggestions: "order this on Blinkit/Zepto: curd, bananas, bread, eggs" and a short cart from the plan.
- Prices, a Rs/day budget, "cheap protein" ranking; whey/protein bar/soya chunks/peanut chikki.
- Cafe/outside food: cafe sandwiches, pizza slices with guidance rather than "needs a kitchen".
- A kettle/microwave kitchen level; hostel-fridge awareness.
- Late-night and exam-week slots; Hinglish language in Ask.
- Teen handling for fast/weight-loss language.
