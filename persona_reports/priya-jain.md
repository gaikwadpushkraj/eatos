## What delighted her
- Dish names say "(Jain)", "no onion", "Kanda-free poha": she instantly trusts them. Hing + cumin as the stated flavour base is exactly how a Jain kitchen does it.
- Wish list tone: "It stays out while the rule applies. Keep it for a day the rule allows" is kind; Pav bhaji (Jain style) listed first for the wish.
- Pantry: onion and potato in the pantry did not tempt the engine to suggest onion/potato dishes. Egg/potato/onion never appeared in any suggestion in any meal slot.
- Profile has a Jain chip, Satvik, "Mumbai street" cuisine, "Rules... are never broken". Taste cards (8): Lauki, Gulab jamun, Ragi dosa, Poha-jain, Chilla, Bajra - friendly, with "Never for me" for a year.
- Clean, fast, no console errors, light-weight, "Hide numbers" option and "Safety floor" copy are respectful.

## Missing for her
- No-sunset rule, and a dinner time computed from sunset; chauvihar / tivihar fast types, ayambil, Paryushan/Das Lakshani, Ashtami/Chaturdashi/Ekadashi green-vegetable-free days (Jains avoid leafy/green vegetables on parva tithis), a Jain calendar for auto-fasts.
- Jain-specific exclusions made visible: she can't see what "Jain" excludes (cauliflower/cabbage/brinjal/fenugreek leaves are avoided by stricter Jains, and Pav bhaji uses cauliflower and Methi thepla uses fenugreek). Offer "strict Jain" level and a "hing instead of onion/garlic" explanation.
- Dabba/office lunch mode: no "carries well/eat cold" tag; Ask "what can I pack for office lunch" returned Moong dal with rice and Lemon rice with no dabba filter. No "family cooks, I just need to know what to ask for" workflow beyond Household.
- Jain Mumbai staples: Jain pav bhaji (present, but only one), Jain Chinese (hakka/schezwan without garlic/cabbage), Jain pizza/sandwich (cucumber-tomato-capsicum), sabudana vada, khichu, handvo, muthia, farsan, kand-free dishes, sweets (shrikhand, mohanthal, ghughra).
- Label guidance: packaged masala/hing/pav bread checks for onion-garlic powder, gelatin, honey (Quick Add of "mushroom" was accepted as a normal pantry item, "Mushrooms · Fridge", with no Jain warning).
