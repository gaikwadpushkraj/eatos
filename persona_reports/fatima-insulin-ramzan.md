# Persona report: Fatima (45, Mumbai, T2 diabetes on insulin, halal, joint family of 5, keeps roza)

Method: built dist driven in Chromium at 390x844 via persona-kit, clock pinned to 2026-10-14 (16:30, 18:45, 04:30, 13:00 IST). Household members added through events. No source edited; throwaway scripts deleted.

## Verdict
- The insulin safety gate holds (no fast is planned) and the wording is polite, but it is a dead end: she is told "EatOS will not plan a fast" and then gets a normal daytime schedule, with lunch at 13:00 and "Drink 500 ml water" prompts, as if she were not fasting.
- Nothing in the app knows what iftar, suhoor, khajoor or Eid are. Wishes for sheer khurma, haleem, nihari, kebab, keema and rooh afza all say "Nothing is in the way" with no tip, which is false comfort for a diabetic.
- Halal has a real bug (sambar and idli-sambar flagged "not halal (drumstick)"), and household conditions (Ammi's BP, Zoya being a child) cannot be entered in the UI and never show as conflicts.

## What delighted her
- Gate is calm, never threatens, and does not force a refusal screen.
- Biryani wish: a sensible "eat vegetables and raita first, smaller rice scoop" tip, and no insulin dosing anywhere in output.
- Jalebi swaps (mango curd cup, fruit and curd) feel kind; "Never for me" on taste cards.
- "Who each dinner works for" matrix with names per person is simple and visible.
- Roasted chana, Ragi malt, Coconut water, Masala chaas suit iftar and suhoor in spirit.

## Missing for her
- Ramzan mode: Ramzan dates, suhoor and iftar time slots (iftar ~18:45, suhoor ~04:30 from sunrise/sunset), no meal prompts in the day, one-tap for the month.
- Iftar logic a real user expects: break fast with water and dates (khajoor) first; light start (fruit chaat, chaas, soup, dahi) before the main meal; taraweeh-time snack; dal/protein suhoor with slow carbs; hydration split across the night (not 500 ml prompts at noon); limit fried pakora, samosa, jalebi to small amounts.
- For insulin users: respectful "talk to your doctor before roza" card with questions (timing, hypo plan), carry-sugar reminder, no doses.
- Eid and festive catalogue: sheer khurma, seviyan/sewai, haleem, nihari, seekh and shami kebab, keema, mutton biryani, korma, phirni, falooda, khajoor, Rooh Afza, jalebi, pakora, dahi vada, with lower-sugar versions (jaggery/dates, less ghee, small katori).
- Household: add conditions, children, and rules to member form; show "Imran: fits; Ammi: salty; Fatima: sweet" soft chips; shared dinner selection should show everyone's trade-offs.
- Meal plan that includes non-veg for omnivores, and a "festival day" override.
