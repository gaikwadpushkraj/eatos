# Persona report: Rohit, 38, Kolkata night-shift call-centre worker

Setup: omnivore, spice 2, Bengali, 390x844 viewport, clock pinned (IST). Night routine seeded as
wake 15:00, breakfast 16:00, lunch 21:00, snack 02:00, dinner 05:00, sleep 09:00 vs the default day routine.
Screenshots: apps/app/persona-shots/rohit_*.png. No page errors, no horizontal scroll at phone width.

## Verdict
1. The app cannot be set up for a night worker: onboarding hard-codes the day routine, so Rohit gets "All done for today. Rest well." at 23:55, mid-shift, and no water reminders at all.
2. Suggestions at 01:30 are sensible in weight (coconut water, banana, light tikka) but ignore Bengali taste and every typed word (biryani, fish, mishti, tea all return the same 3 cards).
3. Catalog has 3 Bengali dishes; wishes for Bengali dishes it lacks say "Nothing is in the way", which reads as success but offers nothing.

## What delighted him
- Fish curry, egg curry and luchi appear by name and as "A taste you grew up with" at his 16:00 and 21:00 meals; wishes for fish curry rice and luchi match instantly.
- At 01:30 Today offers coconut water or banana, not a heavy dish, and "Light for this hour" is true to him.
- "Other options" works, the "+ 250 ml water" and "I already ate this" buttons are one tap.
- 4 h sleep gives a visible, kind note about an earlier wind-down.
- Plan "Cook once, eat twice" and the grocery list (mustard oil, fish, rice) are on target; screens fit a 390 px phone without horizontal scroll.

## Missing for him
- A real "my day" setting (shift/rotating roster, switching between day and night weeks).
- Midnight-proof "today" (wake-anchored day) and hydration reminders on his shifted day.
- Tea/caffeine: quick-log tea, cut-off hour, sugar awareness; chai, muri/jhalmuri, mishti doi, sandesh, khichuri and a real Bengali catalog.
- Honest "I don't know this dish" for wishes and Ask; hour phrases ("at 2am") understood.
- A kind one-liner for the late-night rule (why light, what is fine) rather than a bare tag.
- Bengali bias in Ask and Taste cards.
